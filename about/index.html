FPGA education

Nandland

NANDLAND.com is Russell’s FPGA education site, with free tutorials, videos, and projects that teach digital design in Verilog and VHDL. It also sells the Go Board, a beginner FPGA development board he designed around the Lattice iCE40 HX1K, and his book Getting Started with FPGAs from No Starch Press.
